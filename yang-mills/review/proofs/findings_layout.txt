LAYOUT CHECK OF BEWIJZEN.pdf, 20 November 2026
==============================================

Report of the separate round that looked at a trial print of the PDF built from BEWIJZEN.md
(same AI system as the author; no human reader). Paths starting with <scratch>/ were temporary
working files of that round and are not in the repository.

What was done with the report afterwards: the changes to pdf/make_bewijzen_pdf.py and
pdf/style_bewijzen.css listed under CHANGES MADE were kept; the proposals L1-L6, the first two

SUMMARY
-------
Layout check of the PDF built from BEWIJZEN.md. I fixed the formula and layout defects that could be fixed in the build script and stylesheet; what remains are a handful of badly stretched justified lines that need a long inline formula turned into a display formula in the source (tested replacement text below), plus one stale file reference.

Things to know first:
- The source document changed while I was working (commit e49273c, 13:52 UTC, not by me). The first build (bcb67a2) had 33 pages; the current one has 35. All final checks are on e49273c.
- Nothing is committed or pushed. Only pdf/make_bewijzen_pdf.py and pdf/style_bewijzen.css are modified (+119/-11 lines). No BEWIJZEN.pdf was written to the repository.
- Final trial: <scratch>/pdfcheck/BEWIJZEN_trial.pdf (35 pages). The pre-change build files are saved in <scratch>/pdfcheck/orig/.

Pages actually looked at:
- All 33 pages of the first build at 110 dpi, with 300-900 dpi crops of anything suspicious.
- All 35 pages of the rebuilt current version at 110 dpi, after the main fixes.
- After the last two translation tweaks (upright sans-serif operators; no gap between an operator name and a big bracket) I re-viewed only pages 1, 3, 9, 10, 12, 14, 23, 29 and 34, plus large test renders of the affected constructions. The other pages were not re-viewed after those two tweaks.
- I compared formulas with the LaTeX source where something looked off, not all ~1500 symbol by symbol.

Defects found and fixed (details in changes_made):
- Italic f and j were clipped at the image edge, and every formula carried up to 1 pt of uneven air.
- Primes were tiny and detached; := was set as ": ="; daggers and stars floated; "L ln", "½Tr" and "≥ − ln" were mis-spaced; \mathsf operators were slanted.
- Equation tags sat about 1.7 px low.
- A lead-in line was stranded at the foot of a page, away from its lemma.
- Punctuation could be separated from its formula at a line end.
- English titles in chapter 7 were hyphenated by Dutch rules.

What remains:
- Three lines still have word spaces stretched 5-11x and five more 3.5-4.3x. The proposed source changes were built from a scratch copy (<scratch>/pdfcheck/BEWIJZEN_voorstel.md, giving voorstel.pdf): the 5-11x lines disappear, nothing above 3.8x remains, and the document becomes 36 pages.
- Line 783 names only the round 1 and 2 reports, although the table above it lists round 3.
- The new ink measurement reads matplotlib internals (written for 3.11.2); with a different matplotlib the build stops with a clear message rather than producing wrong images.

CHANGES MADE (build script and stylesheet)
------------------------------------------
- pdf/make_bewijzen_pdf.py, typeset + new ink_extent: each formula image is now cut to the real ink (0.35 pt at the sides, 0.5 pt above and below) instead of mathtext's box rounded up to whole points. Before, 92 of 1732 images had ink outside the canvas (an italic f or j at the edge was cut by up to 0.9 pt) and inline formulas carried up to 1 pt of uneven air, visible as loose or tight spacing next to commas and brackets. Checked by rasterising all 1752 images in Chromium: no ink at an edge, margins 0.25-0.74 pt. The function reads matplotlib internals (3.11.2) and stops the build with a clear message if they differ.
- pdf/make_bewijzen_pdf.py, to_mathtext, primes: u' is now set with the full-size prime directly on the letter (it was a tiny detached mark, as a superscript too); for u'_e and I_n' the subscript is pulled back under the prime.
- pdf/make_bewijzen_pdf.py, to_mathtext, := and =: are now the single signs \coloneq and \eqcolon. They were set as ": =" with a gap, and a long formula could be cut between the colon and the equals sign.
- pdf/make_bewijzen_pdf.py, to_mathtext, ^\dagger and ^\ast are braced, so the dagger or star sits on its letter instead of floating with binary-operator spaces.
- pdf/make_bewijzen_pdf.py, to_mathtext, spacing around operator names: a thin space between a symbol and a following operator name (was "Lln(I1/I2)", "½Tr", "(1-λ)Σ"); no space between an operator name and a sized bracket (was "Tr [", "exp (" next to "Tr("); a minus or plus after a comma or a named relation is braced so it is a sign (was "≥ − ln", "{+1, − 1}").
- pdf/make_bewijzen_pdf.py, to_mathtext, \mathsf{T} is translated to upright sans-serif, as LaTeX sets it. mathtext slanted it, which also made the equals sign crowd the letter ("T= PT= TP").
- pdf/make_bewijzen_pdf.py, insert_math: a formula and the bracket, punctuation mark or hyphen touching it are wrapped in a no-break span, so a comma or closing bracket can no longer start a line.
- pdf/make_bewijzen_pdf.py, insert_math: file names in running text may break after a slash or underscore. This removed the 10x stretched line before "review/proofs/findings_round1.txt" and the one in §6.2.
- pdf/make_bewijzen_pdf.py, new protect_titles: in the literature entries of chapter 7 (list items carrying a ✔ or ○ mark) quoted and italic titles are exempt from Dutch hyphenation. It produced "equati-ons", "conditi-ons", "applicati-ons", "mo-del". Dutch commentary in the same entries is still hyphenated.
- pdf/style_bewijzen.css, span.eqtag: equation tags are centred with a negative margin and line-height 1 instead of a transform; they sat about 1.7 px below the centre of their formula. Now within 0.01 px for all 12 tags.
- pdf/style_bewijzen.css, p:has(+ blockquote) { break-after: avoid }: "Punt 3. We hebben nodig:" was the last line of a page with Lemma B.1 on the next; it now moves with the lemma.
- pdf/style_bewijzen.css, small rules: span.nb (no-break), .titel (no hyphenation), td code / th code nowrap (file names in tables stay whole), img.m.rel margin 0.2em to 0.22em and 0.2em left padding in aligned rows (to make up for the tighter image edges).
