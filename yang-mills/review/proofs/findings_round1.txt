SUMMARY
Stelling A is true as stated and the proof in section 2.4 is mathematically valid: I re-derived every equality and inequality of steps 1 to 8 and found no wrong step, no missing case and no counterexample. All five points are established, including the edge cases: D = 2 (V = 0, M = 1, loop around the ring for dim H_inv = infinity); N >= 3 (not actually needed: the argument works for N >= 2, and N = 2 passes numerically); the range of t (t >= 1 is necessary and correctly imposed, because for a non-gauge-invariant F the component of F*Omega outside H_inv contributes at t = 0 only); real versus complex F; simplicity of lambda_0.

What is not watertight is the bookkeeping around two cited standard results, plus two expository statements:
(1) Point 5 is stated for bounded measurable F, but appendix S3 gives the trace formula only for continuous kernels, and the kernels F(u)T^(t)(u,u') in step 8 are not continuous. The result is standard for L^2 kernels; S3 must be stated in that form.
(2) Appendix S2 (Fubini) is stated only for bounded functions but is applied to unbounded L^2 functions in steps 2, 3 and 4. It is also never shown that P is well defined and bounded on L^2, which a two-line Tonelli argument supplies.
(3) The second remark after the proof says c = min T(u,u') "goes to zero exponentially fast as the number of links grows". That is false in D = 2, where T(u,u') >= 1 for all u,u' by Jensen and c/lambda_0 tends to 1 as L grows. In general c is not a normalised quantity and nothing in the proof supports the claim. What is true is that the lower bound on the ratio min T / max T that the proof yields is exponentially small.
(4) The lay paragraph says m is "what the simulations measure" (and section 5.5 repeats it more explicitly). A simulation measures the decay rate for a chosen observable F, which is the lowest E_n - E_0 with non-zero overlap, hence >= m and in general > m. REPORT.md table 3 itself says the torelon, not the glueball, is the lightest state on six of nine lattices, so there m of Stelling A is below the measured glueball mass. In the Z2 laboratory on a 3x3 spatial lattice psi_1 is a doubly degenerate, centre-odd torelon and the plaquette correlator decays with rate 3.797 against m = 2.574 (beta = 0.4).
The remaining points are cosmetic: the multiplicity convention of the eigenvalue list, the domain of H, and two standard facts used but not listed in the appendix.

The first remark (reflection positivity) is accurate in the usual sense. Sections 1.1 to 1.4, (G1) to (G3), (R1), (R2), and S1, S5, S6 are correct.

The five ref_a_*.py scripts left by the earlier round were read line by line; I found no errors, reran them, and all pass. I added two independent scripts: /home/user/frisoj/yang-mills/review/proofs/ref_a_r2_z2_3x3.py and /home/user/frisoj/yang-mills/review/proofs/ref_a_r2_su2_onelink.py. --- finding A-2 | severity: minor | confidence: high
LOCATION: Section 2.4, step 2(a) (definition of P on H = L^2 and the double integral for <f_1, P f_2>), step 2(c) (the computation of KP = PK), step 3 ("Voor f in H mag de reeks dus term voor term worden geïntegreerd"), step 4 ("(PKf)(u) = int int K(u^g,u') f(u') du' dg"); appendix S2.
CLAIM: P is an orthogonal projection on H, and the order of integration over u (or u') and g may be exchanged for f, f_1, f_2 in H.
PROBLEM: S2 is stated only for bounded measurable functions, but elements of H = L^2(C) are in general unbounded, so S2 as written does not justify these exchanges. It is also never shown that (Pf)(u) = int f(u^g) dg exists for almost every u, that it does not depend on the representative of f, and that ||Pf|| <= ||f||. "P is een orthogonale projectie" presupposes that P is a well-defined bounded operator on H. All of this is true and follows in two lines from Tonelli, the invariance of du under u -> u^g, and Cauchy-Schwarz, but it is not in the text and not covered by the appendix.
PROPOSED FIX: Vervang (S2) door:

"**(S2) Stelling van Fubini–Tonelli.** Voor een meetbare functie op een product van kansruimten die niet-negatief of integreerbaar is, mag de volgorde van integratie worden verwisseld. Dat geldt in het bijzonder voor begrensde meetbare functies, en voor functies van de vorm $\overline{f_1(x)}\, k(x,y)\, f_2(y)$ met $f_1, f_2 \in L^2$ en $k$ begrensd en meetbaar."

Voeg aan het begin van stap 2(a) toe:

"*$\mathsf{P}$ is goed gedefinieerd en begrensd.* Zij $f \in \mathcal{H}$. De functie $(u,g) \mapsto f(u^g)$ is meetbaar, en omdat $u \mapsto u^g$ de maat $du$ behoudt, is $\int\!\!\int \lvert f(u^g)\rvert^2\, du\, dg = \lVert f\rVert^2$ (S2). Voor bijna alle $u$ is $g \mapsto f(u^g)$ dus kwadratisch integreerbaar, zodat $(\mathsf{P}f)(u)$ bestaat, en met Cauchy–Schwarz is $\lvert(\mathsf{P}f)(u)\rvert^2 \leq \int \lvert f(u^g)\rvert^2\, dg$, dus $\lVert\mathsf{P}f\rVert \leq \lVert f\rVert$. Toegepast op het verschil van twee functies die bijna overal gelijk zijn, laat dit zien dat $\mathsf{P}f$ niet afhangt van de keuze van $f$ binnen zijn klasse. In de dubbele integralen hieronder is de integrand integreerbaar, want $\int\!\!\int \lvert f_1(u)\rvert\, \lvert f_2(u^g)\rvert\, du\, dg \leq \lVert f_1\rVert\, \lVert f_2\rVert$; de volgorde van integratie mag dus worden verwisseld (S2)."

--- finding A-3 | severity: minor | confidence: high
LOCATION: Section 2.4, second remark after the proof ("Opmerking (wat stelling A niet geeft)"), the sentence "Het argument in stap 6 gebruikt de constante c = min T(u,u'), en die gaat exponentieel snel naar nul als het aantal links groeit."
CLAIM: c = min T(u,u') tends to zero exponentially fast as the number of links grows.
PROBLEM: False in D = 2 and unsupported in general. (a) By Jensen, T_0(u,u') = int exp((beta/2) sum_e Tr(g_x u_e g_y^{-1} u'_e^{-1})) dg >= exp(0) = 1, because int g dg = 0. In D = 2, V = 0, so c >= 1 for every L. With the spectrum of Stelling B, c/lambda_0 tends to 1 as L grows (beta = 2.3: 0.60, 0.80, 0.95, 0.997, 1.000 for L = 3, 4, 6, 10, 20), and c itself grows like lambda_0. In the Z2 ring c = cosh^L - sinh^L (1.54 for L = 3, 2.15 for L = 4 at beta = 0.7). (b) T is not normalised (w differs from e^{-S} by e^{beta|P|}), so the size of c alone means nothing; the relevant quantity is c/C with C = max T, or c/lambda_0. (c) No estimate of either is given in the proof. What is true and provable: the bounds available from the proof give only c/C >= e^{-2 beta (|E|+|P_s|)}, and a quantitative version of step 6 (Doeblin) gives lambda_1/lambda_0 <= 1 - c/C. The remark's conclusion (no volume-uniform bound) stands; only its justification is wrong.
PROPOSED FIX: Vervang de opmerking door:

"**Opmerking (wat stelling A niet geeft).** De kloof $m$ in stelling A hangt af van $\beta$ en van het ruimtelijke rooster, en het bewijs geeft geen ondergrens die uniform is in het volume. Stap 6 gebruikt alleen dát de kern strikt positief is. Wie het argument kwantitatief maakt, krijgt een ondergrens in termen van $c = \min \mathsf{T}(u,u')$ en $C = \max \mathsf{T}(u,u')$:

```math
\frac{\lambda_1}{\lambda_0} \ \leq\ 1 - \frac{c}{C}, \qquad \text{dus} \qquad m \ \geq\ -\ln\Big(1 - \frac{c}{C}\Big) \ \geq\ \frac{c}{C} .
```

(Bewijs: zij $\psi$ een reële eigenfunctie bij $\lambda_n$ met $n \geq 1$, en $\varphi = \psi/\Omega$. Met $q(u,u') = \mathsf{T}(u,u')\,\Omega(u')/(\lambda_0\,\Omega(u))$ is $\int q(u,u')\,du' = 1$ en $\int q(u,u')\,\varphi(u')\,du' = (\lambda_n/\lambda_0)\,\varphi(u)$. Omdat $\lambda_0\,\Omega(u) = \int \mathsf{T}(u,u')\,\Omega(u')\,du' \leq C \int \Omega$, is $q(u,u') \geq \varepsilon\,\rho(u')$ met $\varepsilon = c/C$ en de kansdichtheid $\rho = \Omega/\int\Omega$. Voor alle $u_1, u_2$ is dan $(\lambda_n/\lambda_0)\,(\varphi(u_1) - \varphi(u_2)) = \int \big[(q(u_1,u') - \varepsilon\rho(u')) - (q(u_2,u') - \varepsilon\rho(u'))\big]\,\varphi(u')\,du' \leq (1-\varepsilon)\,(\max\varphi - \min\varphi)$, want beide termen tussen de rechte haken zijn niet-negatief met integraal $1-\varepsilon$. Neem voor $u_1$ en $u_2$ een maximum en een minimum van $\varphi$; omdat $\psi \perp \Omega$ is $\varphi$ niet constant, dus $\lambda_n/\lambda_0 \leq 1 - \varepsilon$.)

Uit $\lvert V\rvert \leq \beta\,\lvert\mathcal{P}_s\rvert$ en $e^{-\beta\lvert E\rvert} \leq K \leq e^{\beta\lvert E\rvert}$, met $\lvert\mathcal{P}_s\rvert$ het aantal ruimtelijke plaquettes, volgt alleen $c/C \geq e^{-2\beta(\lvert E\rvert + \lvert\mathcal{P}_s\rvert)}$. Dat geeft een expliciete ondergrens voor $m$, maar één die exponentieel klein is in het aantal links en dus niets zegt over oneindig volume. (De constante $c$ zelf hoeft niet klein te zijn: in $D = 2$ is $\mathsf{T}(u,u') \geq 1$ voor alle $u, u'$, wegens de ongelijkheid van Jensen en $\int g\,dg = 0$.) Voor het millenniumprobleem is juist een ondergrens nodig die standhoudt bij oneindig volume én bij $a \to 0$."

Minimale variant: schrap alleen de zin over $c$ en schrijf: "Stap 6 gebruikt alleen dát de kern strikt positief is en levert daardoor geen getal voor $m$."

--- finding A-4 | severity: minor | confidence: high
LOCATION: Section 2.3, paragraph "In gewone taal": "Punt 5 zegt dat m precies de snelheid is waarmee correlaties in de tijdrichting uitdoven; dat is wat de simulaties meten." The same identification appears in section 5.5 (outside my part): "het getal dat de simulaties in het rapport schatten, de kloof m van de overdrachtsmatrix op het gesimuleerde rooster".
CLAIM: The gap m of Stelling A is the number the simulations measure.
PROBLEM: Point 5 says: every C_F(t) is bounded by ||F||^2 e^{-mt}, and one particular F (psi_1/Omega) attains the rate m. For a given observable F the decay rate is min{E_n - E_0 : <psi_n, F Omega> != 0}, which is >= m and equals m only if F overlaps with psi_1. T commutes with the lattice symmetries (translations, rotations, centre symmetry), and psi_1 need not lie in the channel of the measured operator. REPORT.md states this itself: under table 3, "op zes van de negen roosters is niet de glueball maar de torelon de lichtste aangeslagen toestand". On those lattices m <= E_torelon < m_glueball, so the reported glueball mass is not m. Z2 laboratory, spatial 3x3 lattice (hypotheses satisfied): psi_1 is doubly degenerate and centre-odd (torelon); the plaquette operator has zero overlap with psi_1, and its correlator decays with rate 3.797 against m = 2.574 at beta = 0.4, and 1.478 against m = 0.3215 at beta = 0.7. The theorem and proof are not affected; the link drawn to the simulations is too strong.
